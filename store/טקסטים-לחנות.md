## English (optional localization)

**Name (30)**
Malai — Shared Inventory

**Subtitle (30)**
Shared stock lists for families

**Promotional Text (170)**
Manage your home, pantry or small-business inventory together with your family — quantities, low-stock alerts and stock counts, all synced in real time.

**Description**
Malai is a simple app for shared inventory — for the home, the pantry, camping gear or a small business. Every inventory is a list of items with quantities, and any change one person makes shows up instantly for everyone.

What you can do:

• Unlimited inventories — home, pantry, camping gear, office — organized in personal categories.
• Update quantities in one tap, with a full history: who changed what, how much and when.
• Low-stock alerts — set a threshold per item and see at a glance what is running out and what is gone.
• Stock count, item by item — confirm or correct, and everything updates.
• Share with family or your team — add members by username or by scanning a QR code, with per-inventory permissions (who can add, reset, edit or share).
• SKUs, notes and photos for every item.
• Import lists from free text or a file (for example a WhatsApp shopping list), and export to share.
• Undo — deleted something by mistake? You have a few seconds to take it back.
• Manual drag-and-drop ordering, sort by name or quantity, and quick search.
• Works offline — data is stored on the device and syncs when you are back online.

Fully right-to-left Hebrew interface.

**Keywords (100)**
inventory,stock,pantry,warehouse,family,shared list,stock count,supplies,home,storage

**What's New (1.0)**
First release: shared inventories, low-stock alerts, stock counts, QR-code joining and list import from text.
