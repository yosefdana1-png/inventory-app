## English

**Subtitle (30)**
```
Shared stock for home & teams
```

**Promotional Text (170)**
```
New: several accounts on one device, invitations that notify you when answered, and search that finds inventories too. Update together, see every change instantly.
```

**Description**
```
Malai is a simple app for shared inventory — for the home, the pantry, gear or a small business. Every inventory is a list of items and quantities, and any change one person makes shows up instantly for everyone.

What you can do:

• Unlimited inventories — home, pantry, camping gear, office — organized in personal categories.
• Update quantities with a tap, with a full history: who changed what, how much and when.
• Low-stock alerts — a threshold per item, so you see at a glance what is running out and what is gone.
• Stock count, item by item — confirm or correct, and everything updates.
• Share with family or your team — invite by username (they accept from the bell on the home screen, and you're notified of their answer) or by scanning a QR code, with per-inventory permissions.
• Apple Watch — add and remove quantities right from your wrist, even without your iPhone nearby. The watch signs in by itself.
• Several accounts on one device — switch with a tap, no signing out, no retyping passwords.
• Search inventories and items across all your inventories at once.
• Import lists from free text or a file (for example a WhatsApp shopping list), including a low-stock threshold per item, and export to share.
• SKUs, notes and photos for every item.
• Connected devices — see which devices are signed in to your account and sign one out with a tap.
• Undo — deleted something by mistake? You have a few seconds to take it back.
• Works offline and syncs when you are back online.
• Made for iPhone, iPad, Mac and iPhone Duo — on iPad and Mac you see your inventories and the open one side by side.

Fully right-to-left Hebrew interface.
```

**Keywords (100)**
```
inventory,stock,pantry,warehouse,family,shared list,stock count,shopping list,watch,supplies
```

**What's New**
```
What's new in 1.3:

• Several accounts on one device: tap your name on the home screen to switch accounts — no signing out, no retyping passwords.
• Warehouse invitations now need approval: invites wait under the bell on the home screen, and the inviter is notified when an invitation is accepted or declined.
• Apple Watch: the watch signs in automatically from your iPhone, and you can switch accounts or sign out right from the watch. It also appears in your connected devices list.
• Import a list with a low-stock threshold for each item.
• Home screen search now finds inventories too, not just items.
• A new inventory opens right after you create it, and you can add a photo straight from the new-item window.
• Tap the top of the screen to jump back to the top of the list.
• A short guided tour for new users.
• Fixes: a save that fails now shows a message, and the connected devices list is more accurate.
```
